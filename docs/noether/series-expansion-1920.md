# Noether 1920: series expansion in invariant theory

This note maps Emmy Noether's *Zur Reihenentwicklung in der Formentheorie*
to pinned Mathlib. The paper supplies an explicit-operator repair of an
earlier series-expansion argument and appends corrections to two other papers.
It is wholly algebraic: there are no physical interpretations to separate
from the mathematical results.

## Source dictionary

Noether's “Modul” `M` in lines 9929–9947 is the ideal of polynomial relations,
not a modern Lean `Module`. For a substitution
`f : σ → MvPolynomial τ R`, its direct modern model is

```lean
RingHom.ker (MvPolynomial.bind₁ f).toRingHom
```

Thus `F(fᵢ) = Φ(fᵢ)` is exactly the assertion that `F - Φ` belongs to this
kernel. A “normal form” is additional structure: a specially characterized
representative of the quotient class, not merely the quotient class itself.

The coordinates at line 9923 are the two-by-two determinants

```text
pᵢₖ = xᵢ yₖ - xₖ yᵢ.
```

The relation ideal among all `pᵢₖ` is the Plücker relation ideal. In the
three-row specialization of lines 9933–9938, `Δ = (xyz)` is the three-by-three
determinant and the source asserts that the relevant substitution kernel is
the principal ideal generated by `Δ`.

## Three source-authored corrections

These are authorial errata in P16 and must not be classified as transcription
defects in the controlled text of the earlier papers.

### Correction to P08: the old series proof is insufficient

At lines 9953–9957 Noether retracts use of

```text
Ω(Δψ) = c₁ψ + c₂ΔΩ(ψ)
```

as a general identity: it is valid only in the binary case. In controlled P08
the unjustified general use is line 6295. The correction has a precise scope:

- P08 formula (2), lines 6232–6236, whose proof concludes at line 6289,
  survives;
- the reduction theorem (1), concluded at line 6227, survives; and
- the argument at lines 6291–6309 does not establish series (3), because it
  lacks an explicit expression for `F-Φ`.

P16 supplies a repaired route. The correction identifies a proof gap; it does
not assert that the series-expansion conclusion is false.

### Correction to P09: adjoin every conjugate

Line 10061 replaces fields generated by a single element with fields generated
by that element and all its conjugates over `H`:

- controlled P09 lines 7288, 7289, and 7317: `(H,Y)` becomes
  `(H,Y,Y′,…,Y^(λ₀-1))`; and
- controlled P09 lines 7220 and 7289: `(H,y)` becomes
  `(H,y,y′,…,y^(λ₀-1))`.

This is a substantive conjugate-closure/normal-closure repair to the
intermediate-field argument. `IntermediateField.adjoin`, minimal polynomials,
root sets, and `IntermediateField.normalClosure` are suitable substrate, but
the corrected source argument is not already packaged.

### Correction to P11: finite algebraic number field

Line 10063 corrects the “Zahlkörper `Ω`” in the Hilbert irreducibility
statement at controlled P11 line 7916 to an **endlicher algebraischer
Zahlkörper**. “Arbitrary rationality domain” is correspondingly restricted to
a finite algebraic number field with finitely many parameters adjoined, or an
intermediate field of such an extension.

Pinned `NumberField` exactly captures the finite algebraic number-field part:
a field of characteristic zero, finite-dimensional over `ℚ`. It does not by
itself model the added transcendental parameters. `AlgebraicClosure ℚ` and
`IsAlgClosed.splits` supply the premise of Noether's counterexample over the
field of all algebraic numbers; here `AlgebraicClosure ℚ` is a canonical model,
not literally a selected subfield of `ℂ`. Triviality of the associated equation
group still needs the bridge through splitting fields and automorphisms. No
dedicated Hilbert-irreducibility module was found by bounded exact-revision
checks, so the corrected theorem is `DEFERRED_INFRASTRUCTURE`.

## Smallest honest formalization target

The first target should expose only equation [3]'s exact kernel semantics:

```lean
def relationIdeal (f : σ → MvPolynomial τ R) : Ideal (MvPolynomial σ R) :=
  RingHom.ker (MvPolynomial.bind₁ f).toRingHom

theorem sub_mem_relationIdeal_iff
    (f : σ → MvPolynomial τ R) (F Phi : MvPolynomial σ R) :
    F - Phi ∈ relationIdeal f ↔
      MvPolynomial.bind₁ f F = MvPolynomial.bind₁ f Phi := by
  change F - Phi ∈ RingHom.ker (MvPolynomial.bind₁ f).toRingHom ↔
    (MvPolynomial.bind₁ f).toRingHom F =
      (MvPolynomial.bind₁ f).toRingHom Phi
  exact RingHom.sub_mem_ker_iff (MvPolynomial.bind₁ f).toRingHom
```
