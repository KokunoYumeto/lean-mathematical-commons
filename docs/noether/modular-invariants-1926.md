generation as an algebra. It does not mean finite cardinality, and it is not
the same as module-finiteness.

## Main result and assumptions

The theorem at line 14257 states finite generation of the polynomial
invariants of a finite linear group over a field of characteristic `p`, even
when `p` divides the group order. The proof at lines 14319–14335 constructs
orbit-resolvent coefficients, obtains monic equations for the variables, and
passes from integrality to finite generation.

The local theorem uses the following more general modern setting:

- `A` and `B` are commutative rings and `B` is an `A`-algebra;
- `A` is Noetherian and `B` is finite type over `A`;
- a finite group `G` acts on `B` by semiring automorphisms; and
- the action fixes `A`, expressed by `SMulCommClass G A B`.

It requires neither a field, a characteristic assumption, a polynomial ring,
linearity, nor faithfulness. A future concrete matrix-action layer must still
choose the contravariant coordinate-ring convention correctly; using inverses
does not change the fixed ring.

## Mathlib boundary

Pinned Mathlib already contains the essential ingredients:

- `FixedPoints.subalgebra` models the invariant ring;
- `Algebra.IsInvariant.isIntegral` derives integrality from a finite group
  action and its orbit polynomial;
- `Algebra.FiniteType.of_restrictScalars_finiteType` supplies finite type over
  an intermediate algebra;
- `Algebra.IsIntegral.finite` turns integrality plus finite type into
  module-finiteness; and
- `fg_of_fg_of_fg` is the Artin–Tate step that descends finite generation.

No direct declaration combining these ingredients into fixed-ring finite
generation was found in the bounded relevant Mathlib subtrees. Integrality by
itself is not the historical conclusion, so the Artin–Tate step is essential.

## Promoted declarations

`subalgebra_finiteType_of_fixedPoints_le` proves that every intermediate
`A`-subalgebra of `B` containing the fixed subalgebra is finite type over `A`.
Its proof installs the invariant-algebra instance, obtains integrality and then
module-finiteness, and applies Artin–Tate.

`fixedPoints_finiteType` specializes that result to the fixed subalgebra. It is
the characteristic-free modern form of the main theorem and is classified as
`FORMALIZED_GAP`: the combined declaration was missing, while its mathematical
ingredients were already present. This label is not a claim of new mathematics.

The more general intermediate-subalgebra declaration is `NEW_PACKAGING`. It
can finish Noether's relative- and pointwise-invariant conclusions only after
those rings are modeled and their containment of the fixed ring is proved.

## Remaining source targets

The following are not discharged by the two promoted declarations:

- lines 14269–14281: the finite rational-basis theorem and the resulting
  finiteness of algebraic intermediate extensions;
- lines 14285–14299 and 14328: the positive-characteristic
  `P^(1/p) / P` criterion, inseparable integral-closure finiteness, and the
  coefficient-field reduction;
- lines 14312–14326: the explicit universal orbit resolvent, multisymmetric
  row argument, and proof that its coefficients generate Noether's finite
  subring;
- line 14337: a formal ring generated by relative invariants; and
- line 14339: a formal subalgebra of pointwise modular invariants over finite
  fields, including its possible strict containment of absolute invariants.

These remain `GAP_CANDIDATE` or source-specific modeling work. In particular,
the set of relative invariants with varying characters need not itself be
closed under addition; the source conclusion concerns the ring generated by
them.
